### **Vocabulary (Vocabulario)**
- **Normal**: Words ending in al are the same in spanish, but the stress shifts to the last syllable (T2)
- Importante: Words ending in ant and ent are suffixed by an "e"   (Important,adjective) (T3)
	- Differente, urgente
- **Importantemente**:  Adverb of ante adjetive by suffixing with mente (Importantly,adverb) (T3)
- Possible,Probable : Accent last syllable for -ible, -able (T3) + mente
- **-encia / -ancia**: Common noun endings in Spanish, often matching English **-ence / -ance** (T5)
- Opinion : Words ending in ion are feminine and the same but stressed on the final syllable (T4)
- **Clarificación**: Words ending in ==tion== are suffixed with cion Clarification (T4)
- Diversidad : Feminine from english (-ity)
- A -> to direction
### **Verbs & Verb Phrases (Verbos y Frases Verbales)**
- **Cancelar -> Cancelo**: I cancel _(yo form of cancelar)_ (T10)
- **Intento imitar lo**: ==I am trying to== imitate it *(corrected from _Itnennto imitar lo_) (T6)
- **Voy a intentar lo**: ==I am going to== try it *(corrected from _Voy a intentar lo_) (T7)
- Voy a ver los
- **Pasar a**: To pass to / move on ==to==
- Use present to talk about the future -> como -> I will eat
- Sabes que -> You know that
- Lo -> You, Him (formal) vs te
###  Pronunciation Notes
- **G sounds like H** before **e** or **i**: e.g., _gente, girar_ (T3)
- Initial r is double r
- Vowels are spoken like they are spelled
- s is not pronounced like an z
- α,ε,ι,ο,ου
### Grammar Notes
- **"Es"** = "is": From verb **ser**
- **-ar verbs**: Regular conjugation group; many borrowed verbs follow this pattern
- -ar verbs : Verbs from nouns ending in -acion (T4)
- **"No es legal"**: Example sentence using negation and adjective
- Ar,er,ir verbs
- Questions are formed by upward inflecting tone
- Third person plural : add n -> hablan
- Third person er/ir -> puede
- Emmeso first -> they sell it to us -> nos lo venden
